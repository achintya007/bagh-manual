# Installation

## Obtaining a licence

BAGH is not open source. It is distributed under a restricted licence (similar
to CFOUR's): to obtain the program, a research group signs a licence agreement
with the BAGH developers, and then receives the installable package. The
licence allows academic, non-commercial use by the licensed group; the program
may not be passed on to others. Commercial use requires a separate licence.
